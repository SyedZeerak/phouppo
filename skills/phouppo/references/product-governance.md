# Product and Governance Review

Contents: problem fit, usability and adoption, governance.

## Problem fit
- Who is the user and what job does the application perform? Does the workflow match how the business actually operates (plant, sales, distribution, finance), or a generic assumption by the model?
- Requirements traceability: every feature maps to a stated need. Identify invented features and missing requirements.
- Business rules were validated with domain owners and are documented, not inferred.
- Success metrics are defined: adoption, time saved, error reduction, decision quality.

## Usability and adoption
- Onboarding and learning curve for non-technical users; terminology matches what users already say.
- Mobile and field reality: small screens, poor connectivity, shared devices, local languages.
- Workflow completeness: a user can finish an entire task without leaving the app or using side spreadsheets.
- Feedback and support channel, with a response owner.
- Change management: training, rollout plan, handling resistance, and a fallback process during transition.

## Governance
- Product owner named; approval path for changes and releases; backlog and release notes.
- Scope control: the generated app has not drifted beyond the agreed scope.
- Required sign-offs obtained where relevant (IT, information security, HR, finance, legal).
- Stakeholder demo on real data before go-live.
- Exit and maintenance plan, including ownership if the original builder leaves.
